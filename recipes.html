Recipes & inspiration

Fill it with anything.

Sweet or savory, standard or personal — a few ideas to get you started. Every recipe uses the same simple system: place, insert, bake.

PocketCakes

Classic Filled Cupcake

  1. Fill cavities 2/3 full with chocolate cake batter.
  2. Press insert down until batter reaches just below the rim.
  3. Bake per your batter's standard time and temp.
  4. Remove insert while warm. Fill pocket with vanilla buttercream.

Mini Coffee Cake with Fruit Filling

  1. Fill cavities 2/3 full with coffee cake batter, adding crumb topping around the insert rim.
  2. Press insert in and bake as directed.
  3. Remove while warm, fill with pie filling.

S'mores Cupcake

  1. Fill cavities 2/3 full with chocolate cake batter.
  2. Press insert in and bake as directed.
  3. Remove while warm, fill with mini marshmallows and a chocolate drizzle. Broil briefly to toast.

PB&J Cupcake

  1. Fill cavities 2/3 full with yellow cake batter.
  2. Press insert in and bake as directed.
  3. Remove while warm, fill with a peanut butter and jelly swirl.
PocketPies

Personal Deep-Dish Pizza

  1. Press pizza dough into the cavity, about 3/4 up the sidewall.
  2. Press insert in firmly to form the pocket.
  3. Bake until the crust is set and golden.
  4. Remove insert while warm, add sauce, cheese, and toppings.
  5. Return to the oven and bake until cheese is melted and toppings are cooked through.

Chili Cornbread Cups

  1. Fill cavities 3/4 full with cornbread batter.
  2. Press insert in until batter nearly reaches the rim.
  3. Bake per your cornbread recipe's time and temp.
  4. Remove insert while warm, fill with chili.

Mini Chicken Pot Pie

  1. Press pie or biscuit dough into the cavity, up the sidewall.
  2. Press insert in to form the pocket.
  3. Pre-bake crust 5-7 minutes, then remove insert.
  4. Fill with pre-cooked pot pie filling, top with a small dough round, and finish baking until golden.

Personal Bread Bowl

  1. Press bread or pizza dough into the cavity, about 1/2" thick up the sidewall.
  2. Press insert in firmly.
  3. Bake until golden and set.
  4. Remove insert while warm. Fill with soup just before serving.
