4-in-1 bakeware

One system. Four ways to bake.

Every set is a pan and a matching insert that you can use together or on their own. Learn it once and it handles cupcakes, minis, pizzas, pies and more.

01

Pan + insert. The pocket.

Snap the insert into the pan and bake. Lift it out to reveal a ready-made pocket for cream, jam, sauce, cheese or filling. No coring.

02

Two flavors, one bake.

Put one batter in the pan and a second in the insert's space. You get two flavors in every bite from a single trip to the oven.

03

The insert, on its own.

Use the inserts by themselves to make mini bakes.

04

The pan, on its own.

Flexible, non-stick silicone cavities for classic cupcakes, muffins, mini pizzas and pot pies. Pop them out with a gentle push.

How it works

Three steps to the perfect pocket.

1

Add batter or dough

Fill each cavity with cupcake batter, pizza dough or pie crust.

2

Snap in the inserts

Press the inserts in and snap them to the pans. The handles stay above the surface.

3

Bake, then lift

Remove the inserts to reveal a fillable pocket in every bake. Fill with anything you imagine.
